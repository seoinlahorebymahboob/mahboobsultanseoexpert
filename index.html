FAQ

Frequently Asked SEO Questions

Some common questions about SEO and website optimization.

What does an SEO Expert do?

An SEO expert analyzes a website, researches search opportunities and works on technical, content, on-page, off-page and other SEO areas to improve organic search visibility.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term process. The timeline can vary depending on the website, competition, industry, technical condition, content quality and the strategy being implemented.

What is Local SEO?

Local SEO focuses on improving visibility for searches connected to a specific location, helping local businesses become easier to discover online.

Can you help with E-commerce SEO?

Yes. E-commerce SEO can include product pages, category pages, site architecture, internal linking, keyword research and content optimization.

Why is keyword research important?

Keyword research helps identify the language and topics people use when searching. It can help businesses create pages and content aligned with relevant search intent.

Do you provide SEO audits?

Yes. An SEO audit can review technical, on-page, content, structural and other areas of a website to identify opportunities for improvement.
